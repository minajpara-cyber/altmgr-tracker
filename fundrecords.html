Aging of the book

Unrealized value by fund age (report year minus vintage year, USD at ECB quarter-end FX). NAV piling into 6–9 and 10y+ funds is capital that should be coming back — it pressures future realized carry and the LP re-up cycle. Combined vehicles without a vintage (~10% of NAV) are excluded from the split.

Manager trends

Carry at risk = share of unrealized value in funds reporting net IRR below an 8% hurdle proxy (unlikely to be accruing carry). Realization pace = realized proceeds ÷ prior quarter's unrealized value, annualized — the engine of realized performance fees.

Sources & method. Parsed from each manager's 10-Q/10-K "investment record" / significant-funds tables (SEC EDGAR), Brookfield's quarterly supplemental decks, and EQT/CVC report PDFs (qualitative plan-status only — they publish no fund-level numbers). 2017-Q4 onward (TPG from its 2022 IPO; Blue Owl FY2021; Brookfield 2022-Q4; EQT 2025-Q2). Values as reported; EUR/JPY/CAD funds convert to USD only in aggregates. Fund relabels stitched (BEP→Energy, Dyal→GP Stakes, TPG's Angelo Gordon dissolution…). MOIC bases differ by manager (BX/CG total-value MOIC, KKR/TPG gross multiple, ARES/OWL gross & net). IRRs are since inception. "NM" shows blank. Coverage follows what each manager chooses to disclose.
