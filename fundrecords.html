Performance trajectory — improving or worsening?

Manager-level reading of the same fund tables. Weighted IRR drift = quarter-over-quarter change in each fund's since-inception net IRR, weighted by unrealized value — green means the book is being written up, red written down. Carry at risk = share of unrealized value sitting in funds reporting net IRR below an 8% hurdle proxy (those funds are unlikely to be accruing carry). Aging of the book — NAV by fund age

Unrealized value (USD, ECB quarter-end FX) split by fund age = report year minus vintage year. NAV stacking up in 6–9 year and especially 10y+ funds is capital that should be getting returned — an aging book pressures future realized carry, delays the LP re-up cycle, and signals exit-market dependence. Combined vehicles reported without a vintage (~10% of NAV, e.g. Tactical Opportunities "(Various)") are excluded from the age split; coverage is what the managers themselves disclose.

Sources & method. Parsed from each manager's 10-Q/10-K "investment record" / significant-funds tables (SEC EDGAR), 2017-Q4 onward (TPG from its 2022 IPO; Blue Owl from FY2021). Values are as reported: Blackstone in thousands (scaled), others in millions; euro- and yen-denominated funds (BREP Europe, CEP/CEREP, CJP…) stay in their reported currency at fund level and convert to USD only in the rollup charts. Fund relabels are stitched (BEP→Energy, Dyal→GP Stakes, TPG's Angelo Gordon platform dissolution…). MOIC bases differ by manager: BX/CG report total-value MOIC, KKR/TPG gross multiple, ARES/OWL gross & net; the league shows each manager's headline multiple. IRRs are since inception, net of fees where labeled Net. "NM" (too young to be meaningful) shows blank. This is disclosure data — coverage follows what each manager chooses to show, and funds are occasionally bundled or dropped.
