The headline series is the implied net flow: the month-on-month change in fund size that performance does not explain, which is the closest thing these funds disclose to a subscription/redemption number.

Fund size and implied net flow

Bars are the implied net flow for the month; the line is the fund size printed on that month's factsheet. A month whose prior sheet could not be read, or whose NAV moved more than 15%, carries a size but no flow.

NAV per share

Monthly detail

Flow is derived, not disclosed: flow = (size − prior size) − prior size × NAV return. It is a net figure — subscriptions less redemptions less distributions — and it inherits the rounding of the published numbers (size to 0.01m, NAV to 0.01), so read the trend rather than any single month. Audited annual subscription and redemption gross figures are on the Statements tab.

NAV per share by share class

NAV per share for every share class named in the annual and semi-annual reports. Classes differ by currency, fee load and hedging, so two rows of the same fund are not comparable prices.

Audited statements

Annual and semi-annual statement rows, in full units of each fund's own reporting currency. These are the audited gross flows — the figures the implied monthly series approximates between report dates.

The Universe tab shows these same funds with their statements translated to USD. The figures here are untranslated, so the two will differ by FX.
