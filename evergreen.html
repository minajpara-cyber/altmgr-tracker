Fund size and estimated NAV residual

Bars show the estimated NAV residual; the line shows published fund size. Missing, incompatible or unreviewed inputs leave the estimate blank, not zero.

NAV per share

Monthly detail

NAV residual = closing NAV − opening NAV × (1 + monthly return) − other NAV changes. It assumes month-end capital flows. Cash distributions, reinvestment, share-class fees, rounding and capital timing can affect the result. New manager series show estimated external net capital flow only when cash distributions net of reinvestment are verified. Class returns are labeled proxies; source-specific qualifications appear beside each observation. Audited gross flows remain on Statements.

NAV per share by share class

NAV per share for every share class named in the annual and semi-annual reports. Classes differ by currency, fee load and hedging, so two rows of the same fund are not comparable prices.

Audited statements

Annual and semi-annual statement rows, in full units of each fund's own reporting currency. These are the audited gross flows — the figures the implied monthly series approximates between report dates.

The Universe tab shows these same funds with their statements translated to USD. The figures here are untranslated, so the two will differ by FX.
