GP stakes — secondaries position data

Position-level holdings of 17 evergreen secondaries / PE fund-of-funds vehicles, parsed from semi-annual reports (cost + fair value per underlying GP fund) and quarterly N-PORT filings (fair value). Fair value is the GP's own mark (NAV practical expedient). "Entry uplift" is fair value over cost at a position's first appearance — a proxy for the secondary purchase discount. Cost drifts afterwards (returns of capital reduce it, follow-ons increase it), so multiples on aged positions are carrying multiples, not performance.

Value creation — day-one markup vs later appreciation

Each fund's semi-annual gains split into three parts: day-one markup (fair value over cost on newly appearing secondary purchases — buy at a discount, immediately mark at the GP's NAV), held appreciation (mark movement on positions held across both period-ends, net of follow-ons / returns of capital) and realized / unwound (appreciation of exiting positions leaving the unrealized bucket as it converts to realized). The split is validated per period against the fund's own statement of operations (dots on the chart).
