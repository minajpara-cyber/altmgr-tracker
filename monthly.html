Monthly data

Credit BDCs report monthly NAV / distributions / leverage via 8-K; the nt-REITs that skip monthly 8-Ks (BREIT, ARIS, Brookfield REIT) declare each month's per-class NAV in 424B3 pricing supplements. Interval and tender-offer funds disclose monthly subscriptions, DRIP, redemptions, total return by share class, and realized and unrealized gains in their NPORT-P filings. This view consolidates all three sources.
